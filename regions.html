Three architectures. Different bottlenecks.

01 / GRID-FED

Buy delivered electricity.

Study priority: utility allocation, upgrades, tariff and energization sequence.

Watch: backup generation does not replace a missing primary supply commitment.

Explore the grid chapter →
02 / ISLANDED

Carry the whole campus.

Study priority: fuel continuity, generation reliability, black start and plant permits.

Watch: avoiding a load interconnection does not remove the need to engineer resilience.

Test the reliability logic →
03 / PARALLEL

Coordinate two systems.

Study priority: protection, import/export limits, utility permissions and transition controls.

Watch: grid and on-site generation are not automatically additive firm capacity.
